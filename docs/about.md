# About This Handbook

**Windows Forensic Artifacts: A Practical Guide to Evidence Correlation in Digital Forensics and Incident Response** is designed as a practical Windows artifact handbook focused on evidence correlation.

## Editorial Principles

- Investigation first.
- Clarity over complexity.
- Original explanations and examples.
- Distinguish observation, evidence, inference, assumption, and conclusion.
- Avoid unsupported claims about execution, user intent, attribution, and maliciousness.
- Optimize for educational value and professional reporting quality.
